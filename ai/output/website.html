Master BIO 101: Introduction to Cell Biology and stay prepared for every exam.

A clear, step-by-step course breakdown designed to guide undergraduate biology students through key cellular concepts and assessments.

Course Goal and Overview

BIO 101 is a 12-week fall semester course for first-year undergraduate students in biology. The goal of the course is to understand the structure and function of cells, from molecules to whole-cell processes.

12-Week Topic Schedule

Follow the curriculum step by step across the semester:

  • Week 1: What is life? Characteristics of living things, the cell theory
  • Week 2: Chemistry of life: water, pH, carbon and macromolecules
  • Week 3: Proteins: amino acids, folding, enzymes
  • Week 4: Prokaryotic vs. eukaryotic cells, organelles overview
  • Week 5: Cell membrane: structure, diffusion, osmosis, active transport
  • Week 6: Midterm review and MIDTERM EXAM
  • Week 7: Cellular respiration: glycolysis, Krebs cycle, electron transport chain
  • Week 8: Photosynthesis: light reactions and the Calvin cycle
  • Week 9: DNA structure and replication
  • Week 10: Gene expression: transcription and translation
  • Week 11: Cell cycle, mitosis and meiosis
  • Week 12: Final review

Assessment Structure

Grading is divided into three main components:

  • Midterm Exam (Week 6): 30% of total grade
  • Lab Reports: 20% of total grade (4 total)
  • Final Exam (Week 13): 50% of total grade

Final Exam Preparation

The final exam lasts 2 hours and consists of 40 multiple-choice questions and 3 short-answer questions. It covers content from all 12 weeks, with extra weight placed on weeks 7 through 11.
