## Content

- Every block's "source" is copied word for word from the material (one or two sentences). If the material is only a topic name, quote it, invent no facts, and mark anything illustrative as "Beispiel".
- Cover every key idea of the material, including "boring" facts such as units, numbers and named effects. Don't invent facts. Analogies tied to the student's interests are fine if they are clearly examples.
- The first block is a Chunk headed "Warum deine Seite so aussieht": 2 short sentences, addressed to the student, on why this design fits them, naming the evidence with short source names. Its source quotes the profile or the answers.
- Prefer doing over re-reading. When the material allows, use a Lab (factors that change a rate or amount, e.g. photosynthesis: light and CO2; take each slider's effect and numbers from the material, and the notes explain each range in plain words), a Scene3D (a spatial structure, e.g. a leaf or a chloroplast, each part's text quoted from the material), Steps (anything that happens in order) and ReadAloud (for students who struggle with reading). Flashcards only for real vocabulary, never as filler.
- 6 to 9 blocks, each short: a compact lesson, no walls of text. Quiz the common misconceptions, not only definitions.
- All text the student sees is German and addresses the student with "du". Plain, friendly, short sentences. A wrong answer gets a neutral explanation, never blame.
